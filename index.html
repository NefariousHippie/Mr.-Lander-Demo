FAQ

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

No jargon. No confusion. Just clarity.

What exactly do I get with Mr. Lander?
A professional, mobile-friendly landing page, an optimized Google Business Profile, and reliable hosting, maintenance, and security—all set up and managed for you.
Do I have to know anything about websites or technology?
No. You provide your business information, photos, and services—we handle the rest.
How long does it take to get my website live?
Once we have everything we need from you, your landing page can typically be ready within 5 business days.
Can I make changes to my website?
Yes. Your service includes up to two content changes each month, such as updating text, images, or business information.
What happens if I need to cancel my monthly management?
You can cancel your service at any time. Your Mr. Lander website and hosting remain active through the end of your current billing period, after which the service is taken offline. Your Google Business Profile and business information remain yours.
Are there any hidden fees or setup costs?
None. The $499 setup fee covers your entire website build and Google profile optimization. The $99/month covers all hosting, security, and ongoing support. Flat rate, transparent pricing.
Do you offer an annual plan or yearly subscription?
Yes. You can choose the annual plan for $1,000 per year, which works out to $83.33 per month instead of $99 — saving you $15.67 per month, or $188 (15.8%) over the year. You can switch to the annual plan at any time.
How do I know it's working?
Every month, you'll receive a simple analytics report showing how your online presence is performing, including things like website traffic and visibility. Each report also includes a "Mr. Lander's Tip of the Month" with practical ideas to help keep your business visible and growing.
What do you need from me to get started?
Just basic info about your business! We need your logo (if you have one), some photos of your work, a list of your services, your service area, and your contact details. We handle the writing, layout, and technical setup.
