Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 16, 2026

Mr. Lander (“Mr. Lander,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide to us.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we may share it, and the choices available to you when you visit our website, contact us, or use our services.

Mr. Lander is a U.S.-focused service provider based in Virginia and primarily provides services to businesses.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you contact us, request information, become a customer, or work with us during the onboarding and service process.

This may include:

  • Your name
  • Business name and industry
  • Business location
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Information you provide in a message or inquiry
  • Whether you are a new or existing customer
  • Business descriptions and service information
  • Business photographs, logos, and other materials you provide
  • Information necessary to provide and manage our services

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, health information, or similar highly sensitive information through our website.

Payment Information

Payments for Mr. Lander services are processed through Stripe. We do not directly collect or store your full credit card number or other complete payment-card credentials on our own systems.

Stripe may collect and process payment information in accordance with its own privacy policy and terms.

2. Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the Mr. Lander website, certain technical information may be collected automatically by our hosting, security, analytics, or other service providers.

This information may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Approximate usage information
  • Date and time of visits
  • Referring website or page

We use this information primarily to operate, secure, maintain, and improve our website and services and to understand how visitors use our website.

3. Google Analytics and Other Google Services

We use Google Analytics to help us understand website traffic and how visitors interact with our website.

We may also use Google services such as Google Search Console and Google Business Profile in connection with providing services to our customers.

These services may collect or process information in accordance with Google's own privacy policies and terms.

We do not use advertising or retargeting pixels on the Mr. Lander website at this time.

4. How We Use Information

We may use information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and requests
  • Communicate with customers
  • Set up and provide Mr. Lander services
  • Build, host, maintain, and manage customer websites
  • Assist with Google Business Profiles and other online business services
  • Process payments
  • Provide analytics and reporting

  • Maintain and improve our website and services
  • Detect, prevent, and address security issues, fraud, or misuse

We do not sell personal information.

We do not use customer information to train artificial intelligence models.
